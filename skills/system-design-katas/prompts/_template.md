# Prompt NN: Title

## Prompt

What the interviewer says to start. Two or three sentences, deliberately underspecified.

## Hidden requirements

What the interviewer reveals only when asked: users, scope, consistency needs, regions, compliance, what matters most to the business.

## Numbers

Scale figures to give when asked: users, requests per second, read/write ratio, data sizes, latency targets, availability target.

## A strong design covers

The parts a strong answer must get right, especially the genuinely hard part of this problem.

## Probes

Questions to ask about the candidate's design.

## Curveballs

Changes to introduce in the second half.

## Level notes

What separates senior, staff and principal answers on this prompt.

## Pitfalls

Common mistakes.
